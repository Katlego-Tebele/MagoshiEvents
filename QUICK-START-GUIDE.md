# Magoshi Events - Quick Start Guide

Get your website running in 5 simple steps!

---

## 🚀 5-MINUTE QUICK START

### Step 1: Upload Your Logo (2 minutes)
1. Find your Magoshi Events logo (PNG format with transparent background)
2. Save it as `logo.png`
3. Place it in: `assets/images/logo/logo.png`

**Don't have a logo?** Use a beautifully designed text logo temporarily.

---

### Step 2: Upload Event Photos (3 minutes)

Choose your **10 BEST event photographs** showcasing:
- Luxury wedding décor
- Elegant table settings
- Beautiful floral arrangements
- Marquee setups
- Corporate events
- Traditional celebrations

**Save them as:**
- `gallery1.jpg` through `gallery10.jpg`
- Place in: `assets/images/gallery/`

**Quick tip:** Can use the same great photo multiple times initially, then replace later.

---

### Step 3: Upload Background Images (2 minutes)

Choose 2-3 stunning wide photos of your decorated venues:

1. **Hero background** - Your most impressive work
   - Save as: `hero.jpg` in `assets/images/`

2. **About image** - Beautiful event or décor closeup
   - Save as: `about.jpg` in `assets/images/`

3. **Booking background** - Another stunning venue photo
   - Save as: `booking-bg.jpg` in `assets/images/`

---

### Step 4: Upload Service Images (3 minutes)

Choose 6 photos representing each service:
1. Event décor & styling
2. Wedding planning
3. Floral design
4. Marquee & tent hire
5. Furniture hire
6. Corporate events

**Save as:** `service1.jpg` through `service6.jpg`
**Place in:** `assets/images/services/`

---

### Step 5: Test Your Website (2 minutes)

1. Double-click `index.html` to open in browser
2. Click through all sections
3. Test WhatsApp buttons
4. Click gallery images (lightbox should open)
5. Test on your phone

**If everything works:** You're ready to deploy! 🎉

---

## 🎥 OPTIONAL: Add Video

If you have an event video:
1. Save as: `magoshi-events.mp4`
2. Place in: `assets/videos/`
3. Format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
4. Keep under 50MB for fast loading

**Don't have a video?** A placeholder will show. You can add it later.

## 💡 QUICK TIPS

### Image Quality
- **✅ DO:** Use high-resolution photos of YOUR actual work
- **❌ DON'T:** Use Instagram screenshots with UI elements
- **✅ DO:** Compress images to 200-500KB each
- **❌ DON'T:** Upload 5MB+ images (website will be slow)

### WhatsApp Integration
- Every WhatsApp button is already configured
- Uses your number: 082 946 6671
- Pre-fills relevant messages
- Works on mobile and desktop

### Mobile-First
- 70%+ of visitors will use phones
- Test on your actual phone first
- WhatsApp buttons are crucial on mobile

---

## 🆘 QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING

### "Images not showing"
- Check file names match exactly (case-sensitive)
- Verify files are in correct folders
- Make sure extensions are lowercase (.jpg not .JPG)

### "WhatsApp not opening"
- Works differently on desktop vs mobile
- Desktop: Opens WhatsApp Web
- Mobile: Opens WhatsApp app directly

### "Menu not working on mobile"
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser
- Check JavaScript is enabled

### "Website too slow"
- Compress all images (use TinyPNG.com)
- Keep images under 500KB each
- Compress video to under 50MB
